Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  |  App: SolveIt: Math & Logic Puzzle  |  Developer: SAH Labs

SAH Labs ("we," "our," or "us") built SolveIt: Math & Logic Puzzle ("the App") as a free, ad-supported mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our App worldwide, including in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), the United States (including California), India, and other jurisdictions.

1. Information We Collect

No Account or Personal Registration Required: SolveIt does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location. Your game progress, levels, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Automatically Collected Information (via Third-Party Advertising SDK): To keep the App free, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and share certain device-level data, including:

  • Device or other IDs: Google Advertising ID (AAID), device identifiers, and app-set IDs.
  • Device & Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, language settings, IP address (used for approximate country/region-level ad delivery and fraud prevention), and basic ad interaction events (such as ad impressions and clicks).

2. How We Use Information

The limited device data processed through Google AdMob is used strictly for:

  • Displaying banner and rewarded advertisements within the App.
  • Measuring ad performance, frequency capping, and preventing invalid traffic or ad fraud.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements worldwide.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected automatically by the advertising SDK is shared solely with Google LLC (Google AdMob) and its certified ad technology providers in accordance with Google's privacy and security standards. All data transmitted by the SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

For more details on how Google handles data in advertising products, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps that Use Our Services

4. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR): Where required by law, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests your consent before serving personalized advertisements. You may choose between personalized and non-personalized ads, and you can update or withdraw your consent at any time via your device's Google Ads settings.

Users in California and US States (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Under California law, cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing." You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

SAH Labs does not operate backend servers that store your personal data. All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device and is permanently removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime in your Android system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

SolveIt: Math & Logic Puzzle is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided data through third-party services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Security

We value your privacy and ensure that any network communication performed by integrated third-party SDKs uses industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS). However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
